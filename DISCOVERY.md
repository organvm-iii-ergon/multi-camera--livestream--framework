Value thesis (promote): organvm/multi-camera--livestream--framework has real latent value as a productized livestream operations kit for small venues, classrooms, churches, art organizations, and ORGANVM events that need repeatable multi-camera production without hiring a broadcast engineer. The repo's real surface is a shell/YAML/documentation framework: Makefile targets generate active profiles from `software/configs/defaults.yaml` and four budget-to-broadcast profiles, scripts perform macOS setup, health checks, launch, shutdown, and documentation generation, and the docs/hardware/research directories provide runbooks, BOMs, Dante/OBS/NDI guidance, benchmarks, and a GitHub Pages pitch. It is not yet a turnkey web app or subscription product because OBS/Ableton/Dante exports and public pitch copy remain incomplete, but the reusable capability is strong: codify venue-specific streaming setups into profiles, health checks, runbooks, and training material that can be sold as setup packages plus recurring maintenance/monitoring. The best first task is to build a venue readiness/profile generator that takes camera count, audio stack, budget, and platform requirements and outputs the matching YAML profile, BOM delta, pre-stream health checklist, and lead-capture report.
